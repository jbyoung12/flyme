2. The Two Self-Correcting Loops

Loop 1: Ground-Anchored Visual Scene Verification Browser + Gemini 4 Argon

Freehand monocular depth from street video is noisy, so Argon outputs normalized ground-contact coordinates (anchor) and horizon lines (camera_horizons), which server/anchors.js projects onto the ground plane via exact pinhole ray geometry.

Then web/render-views.js renders the 3D scene from all camera poses with color-coded obstacle solids, floating IDs (o1, o2…), and a 5 m metric ground grid. gemini-4-argon inspects each (Footage Keyframe, 3D Render) pair and emits deterministic patch operations until every camera reaches $\ge 80/100$.

Loop 2: Sandbox Simulation & Semantic Choice Managed Agent + Worker Pool

In Agent Mode, the Managed Agent (pre-warmed at SCAN time via client.environments.create({ sources })) proposes 4 diverse routes per round and runs sim-cli.mjs in its Node 22 sandbox. In Search Mode, a worker-thread pool evaluates 400 route shapes (including pure vertical-drop descents) and hands the top diverse SAFE arcs to Argon alongside the street keyframes.

Our simulator enforces 9 deterministic checks (80 kN total / 40 kN horizontal thrust, 500 m/s cold-gas blowdown, 25 kg reserve, 5 m pedestrian blast radius). Argon weighs street semantics (pedestrian paths, sidewalks, overhead wires) to pick and explain the safest route.
